curio rarity game mockups

Three directions, each playable end to end. Every phone frame runs its own game. Use the "Mockup: jump to a screen" button inside each frame to reach the share cards, past days or signup without playing. Sample day: sailing, from Arabic, kitchen, weather, from Persian, instruments, colours. Try typos like flotila or keal.

1. Quiet: curio rarities

Paper puzzle page. Rarity is a mark on a fine scale and the weight of the type. No depth image, almost no motion.

Open full screen

2. Curio with depth: curio dig

Rare words dig down through strata: topsoil, loam, clay, stone, bedrock. Each prompt hides one fossil. The end screen is a section drawing of your seven finds.

Open full screen

3. Full dive: curio depths

The whole screen is the water column, sinking from paper light to brand ink. Depth in fathoms, an air gauge for the timer, a pearl for the gem, bold sinking words.

Open full screen
